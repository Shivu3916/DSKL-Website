Guidelines & Financials

BDA Allotment Rules &
Cost Structure

Essential information on eligibility requirements, Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) rates, allotment priority logic, application timelines, and site cost breakdown.

01

Who Can Apply

Eligibility Criteria
  • Domicile Requirement: Karnataka residents are eligible. Certain categories require valid proof of residence.
  • Income Limits: Specific income caps apply for Economically Weaker Section (EWS) category applicants.
  • Single Application Rule: Only one application per eligible family or individual is generally permitted.
  • Disqualification Rule: Individuals possessing existing ownership of BDA, CITB, KHB, or House Building Cooperative Society sites/houses are ineligible.
02

Earnest Money Deposited (EMD)

Act 1984 · Rule 9

As per BDA (Site Allotment Rules) Act 1984, Rule 9, applicants must submit an initial deposit based on their category:

SC / ST / CATEGORY-1
5%

5% of the total value of the site as initial deposit upon filing application.

GENERAL & OTHER CATEGORIES
12.5%

12.5% of the total value of the site as initial deposit upon filing application.

  • Refund Guarantee: EMD deposit will be completely refunded if allotment is unsuccessful.
  • No Interest Clause: BDA is not liable to pay interest on deposited EMD funds.
  • Adjustment: Upon successful allotment, the EMD amount is directly adjusted against the total value of the site.
03

Procedure of Allotment

Priority Logic

The BDA allotment procedure follows a structured, transparent priority ladder:

#1

Primary Metric — Number of Attempts (Seniority)

Applicants who have applied the highest number of times receive top priority in allotment.

Example: An applicant who has applied 5 times gets priority over an applicant who has applied 4 times.
#2

Tie-Breaker Rule — Age of Applicant

If there is a tie among applicants with equal attempts (e.g. at cut-off of 4 times), priority is given to age.

Example: When site allotment is due for four-time applicants, the senior-most applicant by age will be prioritised first.
04

Time of Applying

Application Window

The official BDA site application window will be open on August 5th and will typically be active for 50 to 60 days.
