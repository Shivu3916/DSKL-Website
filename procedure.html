Four Steps. One Dossier.
Zero Guesswork.

Our structured procedure ensures your application strictly adheres to Karnataka BDA Allotment Rules across all 9 sectors.

Dossier Blueprint Filed to BDA Specification

Eligibility Review

↗

We verify your Karnataka domicile status, category reservation eligibility, income slab, and site holding history.

II

Dossier Preparation

↗
