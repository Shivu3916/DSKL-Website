Sectors 1 – 9 Masterplans &
Architectural Blueprint Portfolio

Explore all nine gazetted sectors of Dr. Shivram Karanth Layout in North Bengaluru, featuring 50x80 villa plots, 40x60 executive plots, central eco-corridors, and civic amenity zones.

Sectors Overview (1 – 9)

Sector 01 · Yelahanka Vista

Sector 1 — 50x80 Villa Estates

Tree-lined 80ft main avenues with underground cabling, rainwater harvesting ducts, and immediate proximity to Yelahanka station.

Sector 02 · Arkavathi Corridor

Sector 2 — Central Park & Green Spine

Over 400 acres dedicated to rain gardens, natural buffer zones, and jogging trails throughout the layout.

Sector 03 · Doddaballapur Hub

Sector 3 — Gazette Approved Blueprint

Comprehensive layout survey with GPS boundary markers, commercial frontage, and arterial transit links.

Sector 04 · Eco Sanctuary

Sector 4 — Bio-Diversity Wetland Reserve

Preserved water bodies integrated into stormwater retention canals and native botanical garden walkabouts.

Sector 05 · Civic Amenities

Sector 5 — Institutional & CA Plots

Reserved BDA sites for primary education, emergency health centers, and community sports complexes.

Sector 06 · Central Plaza

Sector 6 — Commercial & Residential Mix

High-density 30x40 and 40x60 corner plots positioned around the central commercial plaza and retail arcade.

Sector 07 · Villa Estates

Sector 7 — East-Facing Luxury Plots

Exclusive low-density sector designed for 50x80 villa plots with solar street lighting and private park access.

Sector 08 · Executive Enclave

Sector 8 — 40x60 Executive Sector

Quiet residential sector adjoining quiet green buffers, designed for IT and defense personnel quota allotments.

Sector 09 · Metro Boulevard

Sector 9 — Airport Expressway Gateway

Direct connectivity to Kempegowda International Airport Road and proposed Namma Metro Phase 3 station corridor.
